About Us

A Center of Excellence in Science Education

Pasig City Science High School (PCSHS) is a public secondary school in Pasig City, Philippines, dedicated to providing a rigorous science and mathematics curriculum to gifted and talented students.

As a specialized science high school under the Department of Education, PCSHS prepares students to become future scientists, engineers, and innovators who will contribute to national development and the global knowledge economy.

Research & Innovation

Advancing Knowledge, Shaping the Future

At PCSHS, research is not just an activity — it is a core part of our identity. Our students engage in real scientific inquiry, guided by expert faculty mentors, and present their findings at regional, national, and international platforms.

From biology to computational science, our research program has produced award-winning studies and positioned PCSHS as a leading science secondary institution in the Philippines.

Admissions

Every year, PCSHS admits the most talented and passionate young minds from Pasig City and nearby areas. Our rigorous selection process ensures that admitted students are ready to thrive in our demanding science-oriented environment.

01

Secure Application Form

Download or pick up the application form from the school registrar or our website.

02

Submit Requirements

Submit completed form with required documents before the deadline.

03

Take the Entrance Exam

Sit for the PCSHS Entrance Examination covering Math, Science, and English.

04

Check Results & Enroll

Successful applicants are notified via the school portal and proceed to enrollment.

Apply Online Now

Requirements

  • Duly accomplished application form
  • Grade 5 & 6 report card (SF9)
  • Recent 2x2 ID photo (3 copies)
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • Certified true copy of awards (if any)
  • Medical clearance

Key Dates 2025

  • Applic. PeriodJan 6 – Feb 14
  • Entrance ExamFeb 22, 2025

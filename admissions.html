Admission at a Glance

PCSHS admits only the most talented and passionate young minds from Pasig City who meet our rigorous academic standards.

85% Minimum Grade Average
STEM Only Strand Offered (SHS)
Free No Tuition Fees

Eligibility

General Eligibility Requirements

Who Can Apply?

  1. 1
    Must be a Filipino Citizen.
  2. 2
    A Grade 6 pupil from any DepEd-recognized public or private elementary school who is a candidate for graduation for the current school year and has not yet enrolled in any secondary school.
  3. 3
    Must have obtained the following minimum grades:
    • For Grade 5: final grade of at least 85% in English, Mathematics and Science, and 83% in all other learning areas.
    • For Grade 6: grade of at least 85% for the first and second quarters in English, Mathematics, and Science, and 83% in all other learning areas.
  4. 4
    Must possess good moral character, certified by the school principal or guidance counselor.
Important Note: A student can only apply to one Science High School within the National Capital Region. For schools using letter or descriptive grading systems, a certification of numerical equivalents must be provided.

Proof of Residency

Applicants may submit a photocopy of ANY ONE of the following:

  • Valid ID with residential address
  • Latest Voter's Certification
  • Latest Utility Billing Statement (under parent/guardian's name)
  • Latest Real Property Tax Receipt
Process

Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 01

Evaluation of Submitted Documents

Original and photocopy documents must be placed in a long brown envelope. Write the following details of the student at the LEFT SIDE of the brown envelope:

📋 Sample Envelope Label Format: INCOMING GRADE 7 – PUBLIC
DELA CRUZ, JUAN SANTOS
MALE / 0912-345-6789
PASIG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Submission of Documentary Requirements shall be submitted directly to the Registrar's Office from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday to Friday only).

Step 02

Administration of Qualifying Examination

A computer-based examination shall be given to student-applicants. Before the conduct of examinations, students and parents are required to attend an orientation.

The results of the examination shall be released after a considerable span of time. Students will be classified into categories:

Category A Score 75% and above
Category B Score 60%–74%
Category C Score below 60%
Step 03

Interview

Students classified in Category A will automatically be included in the interview. Students from Category B will be ranked, and those included in the cut-off/absorptive capacity shall be eligible for interview.

The on-site interview holds 20% of the overall qualification requirements.
Step 04

Results

A total of 245 students will be admitted to Grade 7 based on the final ranking determined by:

  • 80% — Examination results
  • 20% — Interview score

Results are posted through DepEd NCR's website and Facebook page, the school's designated posting place, Official Facebook Page, and website, subject to compliance with the Data Privacy Act.

Enrollment Condition: Qualified applicants must present the FINAL Grade 6 report card, showing compliance with the required grades in all grading periods as a condition for confirmation of enrollment.

Step 05

Confirmation and Enrollment

Applicants who are included in the final roster must confirm their acceptance and enroll within five (5) working days from the official release of results.

Failure to confirm within the deadline shall be considered a waiver of the slot, and the slot shall be offered to the next qualified applicant in the waiting list.

Required Documents

  • Completed Application Form (Grade 7 or Grade 11)
  • Latest Report Card (SF9/Form 138)
  • Must have a final average of at least 85%
  • No grade lower than 80% in any subject
  • PSA Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 2×2 ID Photos (at least 3 copies)
  • Proof of Residency in Pasig City (any one)
Place all documents in a long brown envelope with student details written on the left side.

Key Dates

  • Application Period: To be announced
  • Submission: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
  • Orientation: Before exam date
  • Results: Posted online + on-site
  • Confirmation: 5 working days from results
Watch the official Facebook page for official dates and announcements.

For SHS Applicants

  • Only the STEM strand is offered at PCSHS
  • No transferees accepted
  • Only incoming Grade 7 & Grade 11 are accepted
  • Tuition is completely free

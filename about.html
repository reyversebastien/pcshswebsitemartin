Welcome to PCSHS

Welcome to Pasig City Science High School (PCSHS), a premier public science high school dedicated to excellence in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). Serving both Junior High School and Senior High School students, PCSHS provides a focused academic environment that challenges learners to reach their highest potential. Our STEM-centered curriculum strengthens analytical thinking, innovation, and problem-solving skills. We cultivate discipline, integrity, and leadership among our students. At PCSHS, we prepare future scientists, engineers, and professionals to contribute meaningfully to society.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

🚀

Mission

PCSHS is committed to fully developing well-rounded, Science and Technology-oriented students — nurturing gender-sensitive persons and future leaders with a vision for excellence.

🌏

Vision

Pasig City Science High School (PCSHS) is envisioned to be a world-class institution that is globally and locally competitive and responsive to the ever-changing needs of the times, especially in the fields of Science and Technology.

⭐

Core Values

  • Excellence in Science and Technology Education
  • Integrity and Moral Uprightness
  • Innovation and Creative Thinking
  • Service to Community and Nation
  • Patriotism and Filipino Identity
School Objectives

Our Primary Objectives

As a premier public science high school, PCSHS is guided by these core institutional objectives.

  1. Offer scholarship grants to deserving gifted students in Pasig City who demonstrate exceptional aptitude in Science and Mathematics.
  2. Provide a high quality curriculum and instruction enriched with varied Science and Technology courses beyond the standard K-12 program.
  3. Emphasize high standards of learning and high levels of learning skills such as critical and creative thinking and problem solving.
  4. Provide state-of-the-art facilities that enhance interactive and hands-on learning experiences for all students.
  5. Harness to the fullest the artistic, athletic, managerial and/or leadership talents of the students as well as their conscientious stewardship of the environment.
  6. Inculcate among the students the values and virtues in life necessary in their interrelationships with selves, others, country, and God.

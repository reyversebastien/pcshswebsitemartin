Requirements Checklist

Please gather the following documents. Originals + 1 photocopy each are required upon submission. Check each item you have ready.

  • PSA-certified Birth Certificate (original + photocopy) REQUIRED
  • Report Card / Form 138 (Grade 5 & current grade) REQUIRED
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character (current school principal) REQUIRED
  • 2x2 ID photo (white background, name tag, 4 copies) REQUIRED
  • Photocopy of parents' valid government ID (any 1)
  • Medical/Health Certificate (issued within 6 months)
  • Awards & Recognition documents (if applicable)
  • Barangay Clearance or Certificate of Residency

Physical documents must be submitted to the PCSHS Registrar within 5 days of exam result release.

Eligibility

  • Currently in Grade 6 or Grade 10
  • General Average of 85% and above
  • Resident of Pasig City (priority)
  • No failing grades in any subject
  • Good moral character (no major offenses)

FAQ

Is tuition free?

Yes! PCSHS is a public school under DepEd. Education is completely free including textbooks.

What is the exam like?

The entrance exam covers Science, Math, English, and Abstract Reasoning. Duration is 2.5 hours.

Can non-Pasig residents apply?

Yes, but Pasig City residents are given priority. Slots for non-residents depend on availability.
